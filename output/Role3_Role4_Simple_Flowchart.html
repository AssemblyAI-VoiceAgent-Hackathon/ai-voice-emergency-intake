AI Emergency Intake Role 3 and Role 4 — a simple picture for non-technical teammates

The front desk and the records room

Think of a hospital. Role 3 is the front desk (Mozzam). Role 4 is the locked records room (Ishaan). Staff never walk into the records room themselves. Everything goes through the front desk.

Role 3 — The front desk · Mozzam

  • Checks the badge. Is this person allowed to send or review this case? The computer does not trust a name typed on a form.
  • Checks the paperwork. Is the draft complete? If not, it is sent back with a plain reason.
  • Keeps everyone in sync. When a new draft arrives, the staff screen updates live.
  • Hands work to records. Asks Role 4 to look someone up, and later to file an approved case. Role 3 does not keep the final file itself.

Role 4 — The records room · Ishaan

  • Looks up the patient. Have we seen this person before? Answers: found, not found, or the notes disagree.
  • Files the approved case once. Only after a clinician approves. The same request again does not create a second file.
  • Keeps a locked diary. Who changed what, and when. That diary cannot be quietly edited or deleted.
  • Protects private details. Sensitive words are locked. Dispatchers see less than clinicians.

Who talks to whom

The staff screen never writes to the records room. The AI never writes there either. Only the front desk is allowed in.

The story, start to finish

1

A draft case arrives

The voice call becomes text. The AI turns that into a structured draft: what hurts, what is missing, what disagrees, and what staff should notice. That draft is sent to the front desk.

2

Front desk checks three things

Who is sending this? Is the form complete? Is this a newer version than the one we already have? A repeat of the same request is treated as “we already have this,” not as a second case.

×

Sent back

Wrong person, incomplete form, or an older version. Staff see a clear message, not a crash.

3

Accepted — screen updates

The live staff screen shows the draft, gaps, conflicts, and confidence. No refresh needed.

4

Need history? Ask records

“Do we already know this patient?” Role 3 asks Role 4. The staff screen is not allowed to ask the records room directly.

5

Records room answers

Found — send the notes (clinicians see more; dispatchers see less). Not found — say so clearly. Notes disagree — flag it for a human. The answer goes back through Role 3, then the AI can update the draft.

6

Staff review the draft

A clinician can save a draft, ask for more information, or approve. They can correct fields. Those corrections are protected so a later AI update does not wipe them.

7

Front desk checks the reviewer

Identity comes from the signed-in badge, not from a name in the message. If the case changed while they were reading, they are asked to look at the latest version instead of overwriting someone else’s work.

8

If they approve — hand to records

Only then does Role 3 ask Role 4 to file the finished case, the triage the human chose, and the list of corrections.

9

Filed once, with a locked diary

The approved record is stored. Sensitive details are locked. The diary records who approved, what changed, and when. The same request again returns the original file — it does not create a duplicate.

10

Staff screen is told “saved”

The front desk publishes the result. The reviewer sees that the case is approved and filed. The loop is closed.

Three things everyone should remember

The computer does not decide

The AI draft is a helper. A qualified human still checks, corrects, and approves. There is no automatic diagnosis or final triage.

One door into the records

The staff screen and the AI never write to the database. Role 3 checks the person, then Role 4 files the record.

Practice data only for now

Names look like TEST-PATIENT. Notes start with SYN:. Real patient details, recordings, and passwords stay out of GitHub.

If you remember only one picture

Call → AI draft → Role 3 checks it and shows it live → staff review → Role 3 checks the reviewer → Role 4 files it once and writes the diary → staff see “saved”. Role 3 is the door. Role 4 is the vault. A human still makes the final call.
